```scss
count_equations(List, N) :-
    aggregate_all(count,
        ( split_numbers(List, LeftNumbers, RightNumbers),
          expression(LeftNumbers, _, Value),
          expression(RightNumbers, _, Value)
        ),
        N).
```

**Why this works:**

- `aggregate_all/3` counts valid expression-tree pairs directly, avoiding string formatting, list construction and sorting.
- Both expressions share `Value`, ensuring their evaluated integer values are equal.
- Repeated input numbers do not inherently cause overcounting because `split_numbers/3` enumerates cut positions and `expression/3` generates operator-labelled trees, rather than permutations of the input.
- Each distinct expression-tree pair corresponds to a distinct canonical equation string, so the count matches `all_equations/2` under the assignment's formatting rules.

**Complexity improvement:** This avoids the overhead of constructing and sorting hundreds of thousands of strings, consistent with your observed reduction from approximately 7.7 seconds to 0.49 seconds.
